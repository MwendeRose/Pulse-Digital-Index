Who It's For

Grounded in field research

PDI is designed around the real people who use digital services and the problems they face. Each profile is a working hypothesis that we confirm with field evidence.

“Did it work?”

Retired, Low Digital Literacy User

Needs unmistakable confirmation. Won't trust a subtle icon. Lives on a fixed income, so mistakes feel costly.

The barrier: They repeat actions, ask for verification, or skip digital entirely. Small text and no clear path to a human make it harder, and help that talks down to them costs trust.
“This feels clunky”

Tech-Native Student

Benchmarks everything against fintech apps. Wants speed, social features, and visible progress toward goals.

The barrier: Slow loading or clunky onboarding feels like a product failure, so they abandon fast. With no way to split payments or save with friends, they fall back to WhatsApp or M-Pesa.
“What’s my margin?”

SME Owner

Thinks in profit and loss daily. Every fee and every friction point feels like a direct cost to the business.

The barrier: Can't see profit at a glance or keep business and personal money apart. Fixed repayments and fees don't flex with seasonal cash flow.
“Can I audit this?”

Corporate Accountant

Every number may be audited later. Needs approval workflows and clear audit trails for large transactions.

The barrier: Missing approvals force workarounds and leave them personally exposed. Slow bulk actions compound at scale.
“What will they get?”

Diaspora User

Can't verify in person. Fees and rates matter because they determine what actually arrives. No branch fallback.

The barrier: Can't verify the fee, rate or timeline before sending, or confirm the transfer landed. Trust is built entirely on screen.
